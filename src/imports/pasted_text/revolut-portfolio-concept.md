Case Study lineup (fintech-forward, 3-project slate)

Case Study A: GuardianOnboard — Fintech onboarding with identity, KYC, and trust

Context: Launching a multi-currency wallet for new customers; onboarding friction was causing drop-offs.
Your role: Design lead for end-to-end onboarding, coordinating with Product Owner, Engineers, Data Scientist, and Compliance.
Problem framing: Identify friction points in identity verification, data privacy concerns, and messaging clarity.
Approach:
Research: interviews with new users, journey mapping, heuristic review focused on trust signals.
IA & Flows: simplified KYC flow with progressive disclosure, real-time validation cues, privacy explanations.
Prototyping: high-fidelity prototypes with accessible components and micro-interactions to reassure users.
Validation: Usability testing, A/B micro-variants to messaging and progress indicators.
Impact (target metrics you can anchor):
Activation rate up by 12–20%; onboarding time reduced by 20–30%; fewer support tickets on KYC by 15–25%.
Handoff: design-system tokens, shared reusable components, developer handoff notes with validation criteria.
Visuals to include: annotated onboarding journey, decision logs, real-time verification UI, accessible labels.
Case Study B: QuickPay — Cross-border transfers with real-time transparency

Context: Cross-border transfers within the Revolut ecosystem; users want clarity on fees, exchange rates, and ETA.
Your role: End-to-end designer for transfer journey; collaboration with Product, Engineering, Data, Compliance.
Problem framing: Reduce anxiety about fees and ETA; prevent failed transfers due to friction with regulatory checks.
Approach:
Research: user interviews about transfer pain points; map out pain points in currency selection, rate visibility, ETA.
Design: real-time rate previews, transparent fee breakdowns, ETA indicators, clear success/failure messaging.
Prototyping: clickable transfer flow with status chips, progressive disclosure for details.
Validation: usability tests; measure trust signals and drop-offs at confirmation.
Impact: increase transfer completion rate; reduce post-transfer inquiries by clarifying expectations.
Handoff: emphasis on rate APIs, error-first messaging patterns, and a small design system extension for transaction modals.
Case Study C: FinSight Design System — Scalable components for a multi-product fintech

Context: Several products (accounts, transfers, cards, investments) lacked a unified design language.
Your role: Design System Lead; collaboration with Product squads, Engineers, DesignOps, Accessibility.
Problem framing: Inconsistent UI, long delivery cycles due to duplicated effort.
Approach:
Audit: inventory existing components; identify accessibility gaps; define tokens and scalable patterns.
System design: create a modular, accessible set of components with documentation; emphasis on WCAG compliance.
Governance: lightweight guidelines for contribution, versioning, and cross-squad adoption.
Validation: pilot with 3 squads; track delivery speed and UI consistency improvements.
Impact: faster design-to-dev handoff; improved accessibility conformance; consistent user experience across products.
Handoff: design tokens, component library, governance doc, and a living style guide.
